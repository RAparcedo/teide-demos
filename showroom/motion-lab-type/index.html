Headlines and text, rebuilt our way.

Twelve type effects with ideas from Magic UI, React Bits, daisyUI, Kokonut UI, Fancy Components and others, rebuilt in plain CSS and a few lines of script. No word hides if a script fails, and every effect holds still for people who ask their phone for less motion.

Scroll through on your phone, then send me the codes worth keeping.

T2, T3, T7 and T8 follow your scroll. They run in Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet and Safari 26 or newer; in older browsers you see the finished text, with nothing missing. T9 only moves with a mouse or keyboard. The businesses are the fictional showroom ones, and every price, date and time is an example.

T1

Rotating word in a headline

One word in the headline swaps between three services, goes round three times, then stops on the first. Screen readers hear one plain sentence. For barbers, salons and trades with a few headline services.

  • Small shared script
  • Plays once, 3 rounds
  • Idea: daisyUI, Magic UI

T2

Marker and circle that draw on view

A highlighter swipes behind the key phrase and a pen circles the price as they scroll into view. The finished marks are the default, so nothing depends on the motion. For "sin cita previa", "24 h" or an offer price.

  • 0 KB JavaScript
  • Scroll-linked
  • Idea: Kokonut UI, Rough Notation, Magic UI

T3

Paragraph that inks in as you read

Each word darkens from a soft brown to full ink as the paragraph scrolls up, as if the pen were catching up with you. The soft brown already passes contrast, so it reads fine before it inks. For an about-us paragraph on a casa rural or a family restaurant.

  • 0 KB JavaScript
  • Scroll-linked
  • Idea: Magic UI, Bramus

T4

Photo pill between headline words

A round-ended photo sits inside the headline like a word. It opens from a dot to a full pill once, and its space is kept from the start, so nothing jumps. For a restaurant, bakery or florist headline.

  • Plays once
  • Idea: Fancy Components

T11

Breathing variable-font word

Each letter swells from thin to heavy and back in a wave, three breaths, then rests. Every letter keeps the space of its heaviest weight, so the word never jiggles. For a spa, a yoga studio, or a bakery's "pan".

  • Small shared script
  • Plays once, 3 breaths
  • Idea: Fancy Components, Eldora UI

T12

Text riding a wave

Words follow a wave line under the photo and drift slowly along it, with a pause button. The curved text is complete without the drift. For surf and kite schools, boat trips and beach bars.

  • ~1 KB script
  • Drifts, with Pause
  • Idea: React Bits, Fancy Components
