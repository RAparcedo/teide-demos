The local

Not a chain. Not a gimmick. Just your bar in the sun.

The Eldon has been a Playa Paraíso local since 1998 — and since 2020 it's been in the hands of sisters Corinne and Carly, with Corinne's husband Simon. Family-run in the truest sense, and they've kept it exactly what it should be: the little hide-away regulars call a "gem," with a warm welcome every single time.

No frills, no fuss: honest prices, a proper drink whenever the mood takes you, and a lovely terrace looking straight out over the ocean — open all day, and the best seat in the house when the light turns gold.

Drinks only — but you won't go hungry. There's no kitchen here, though you're very welcome to order food from the bar next door and enjoy it with us, drink in hand.
